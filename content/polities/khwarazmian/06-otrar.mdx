In 1218 a trade caravan under Mongol protection arrived at Otrar, on the
Khwarazmian frontier. The governor, Inalchuq, had the merchants killed as spies
and kept the goods. Chinggis Khan sent envoys to demand redress. Ala al-Din
Muhammad had the senior envoy killed and the others sent back shaved.

The invasion arrived in 1219 and it took about two years.

## The decision that lost it

Muhammad had a large army and chose to distribute it among his cities rather
than concentrate it and fight, on the reasoning that the fortified places of
Transoxiana could not all be taken at once.

It was not a stupid calculation against any enemy he had previously met. It was
catastrophic against this one, because it turned a field campaign into a
sequence of sieges, and the Mongols had brought a thousand Chinese engineers
with catapults. Otrar, Bukhara, Samarkand and Gurganj were reduced one after
another. Nishapur, Merv and Herat followed.

The destruction in Khurasan was of a different order from anything the region
had seen. Cities that had been continuously central since the Tahirids — that
had held the libraries, the madrasas and the canal systems — stopped being
central, and several of them stopped existing.

## The flight

Muhammad did not stay to be besieged in any of them. He went west with the
Mongol columns behind him, across northern Iran, losing the country a province
at a time, and died of pleurisy on an island in the Caspian at the end of 1220
with no territory left at all.

He had ruled the largest state in the eastern world three years earlier. He was
buried in borrowed linen because there was nothing else.

That is the end of the Khwarazmian empire as a state. What follows is one man.
