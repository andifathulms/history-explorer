Kediri's `peak` ruler is Jayabhaya, who reigned from about 1135 to 1157, and the
reason he is the peak is not a battle or a boundary.

It is the *Bharatayuddha*, an Old Javanese kakawin composed at his court in 1157
by the poets Mpu Sedah and Mpu Panuluh, which takes the war of the Mahabharata
and makes it Javanese. It sits at the head of a body of court literature — the
*Smaradahana*, the *Kresnayana*, the *Hariwangsa* — that this kingdom produced and
that everything written in Java for the next seven hundred years was measured
against. Majapahit's *Nagarakertagama*, which this corpus's Majapahit record
devotes a chapter to, is in the same tradition and the same metres.

The *Bharatayuddha* is still performed. The wayang repertoire of Java and Bali is
built on it.

## And this site has nowhere to put it

Look at what the influence block on this record can say. Three counts: descendant
scripts, zero. Religions carried, zero. Successor claims, zero.

Kediri produced no script — Kawi descends from a South Indian Brahmi, and coding
rule 3 sends that count to a parent with no page here, which is now the sixth
time on this site an adaptation has gone nowhere, alongside Jawi at Melaka,
Swahili's letters at Kilwa, the Burmese script at Pagan, the Cham script, and
Pegon on the Demak record two pages over. It carried no religion; it received
Shaivism and Buddhism like everything else in Indic Java. And nobody claimed it:
Singhasari took its territory by force and did not pretend to be it.

So a kingdom whose actual bequest to the region was a literature reads on this
site as three zeroes.

This corpus's Champa record ends its script comment by saying that the Đông Yên
Châu inscription is the oldest text in any Austronesian language and that this
site has no measure for being first. This is the same absence from the other
direction. The three influence counts were chosen because they can be applied
uniformly to a hundred and ninety-nine records — a script either has descendants
or it does not, a religion either crossed a boundary or it did not — and
literature cannot be counted that way without becoming an opinion wearing a
number, which is what the coding rulebook exists to prevent.

The rulebook is right and the record is still poorer for it. This chapter is
where that goes.
