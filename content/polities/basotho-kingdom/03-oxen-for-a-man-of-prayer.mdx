Word of the Basotho kingdom reached the outside world through its own
victims. Koranna and Griqua raiders armed with muskets had been
plundering tribes along the upper Caledon in complete secrecy, until
some of those they had ravaged tracked their persecutors' own route
and, in despair, resolved to die near the flocks stolen from them
rather than live without hope. Among the very people their raiders
belonged to, they instead found men moved to kindness by their story -
one of whom had himself been taught by English missionaries, and told
the grieving refugees plainly that only the Christian faith could
restore peace to their country. Moshoeshoe, hearing of the encounter,
summoned the man in person, was persuaded, and before the visitor
left secured a promise that missionaries would be sent. When time
passed without word, Moshoeshoe sent oxen after him with a plain
request attached: procure him, in exchange, "a man of prayer."

## A country littered with the evidence of its own ruin

Casalis and his companions, newly arrived at the Cape and still
uncertain where to begin their own mission, heard the story from Dr.
Philip, the missionary and reformer who had done more than anyone to
protect the Cape's indigenous peoples, and set out at once to find the
Basotho who had carried it to the coast. Six months and two hundred
leagues after leaving Paris, their ox-wagon crossed into a landscape
the wars and famines of the previous decade had left almost
uninhabited - human bones whitening in the open on every side, the
occasional hunter diving into the grass at the sight of their caravan
rather than risk being seen at all.

## A welcome that changed as the mountain came into view

The closer the party came to Moshoeshoe's own residence, the more the
land transformed: armed shepherds tending real flocks, fields
visibly under cultivation, villagers coming out to see the white men
rumored to be able to "repair all past disasters," bringing pumpkins,
milk and cane in curious welcome. It was, in miniature, the whole
story of Moshoeshoe's own reign made visible in a single day's
approach - devastation near the frontier giving way, the nearer one
came to the mountain, to a kingdom still standing amid the ruin of
everything around it.
