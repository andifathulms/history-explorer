title: What six hundred and ninety-eight years means

The Bavandids ran from 651 to 1349, which is six hundred and ninety-eight years
and makes them one of the longest-lived dynasties anywhere in the medieval
Islamic world — longer than the Ottomans managed.

The figure encloses two periods, of sixty-seven and twenty-seven years, in which
the family ruled nothing at all.

## Whether that counts

Historians answer this kind of question two different ways, and the difference is
not carelessness on anybody's part.

The Zaydi imams of Yemen are conventionally given a single span from 897 to 1962,
a thousand years with substantial interruptions inside it, because Yemeni
historiography treats the imamate as one institution that was sometimes in
abeyance. The Ibadi imamate of Oman is counted the other way: the same office was
reconstituted in the same mountains repeatedly across a millennium, and Omani
historiography numbers those imamates in the plural, as separate constitutions
rather than one thing with gaps.

The Bavandids belong with the Yemeni pattern, and for a reason about the sources
rather than about tidiness. The three Bavand lines are one family with a
continuously asserted descent, named collectively as a single dynasty by the
works that record them. Three lines of one house are not three polities in the
way that two Omani imamates constituted by two separate elections are.

## What the number cannot say

Six hundred and ninety-eight years, stated flatly, implies a continuity that a
reader should know was interrupted twice. Any single figure for how long
something lasted assumes the thing had one beginning and one end, and a family
that loses its mountains and comes back is not that shape.

It is the second measurement on these pages that strains in this way. Territorial
extent cannot describe Srivijaya, which held a strait rather than an area, or the
Nizaris, who held scattered castles and nothing between them. Duration cannot
describe a polity that stops and starts. Both measures were built for states with
a single life, and the better course is to give the number with the argument
attached than to leave out the polities that break it.
