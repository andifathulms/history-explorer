This record's `religions_carried` count is zero, on a page about ten thousand
Buddhist temples. That is going to look like an error, so here is why it is not.

Coding rule 2 counts propagation past a prior confessional boundary. Not
adoption, not devotion, not scale of investment: carrying the religion somewhere
it had not been. The rule was written so that the count would mean the same
thing on every record, including the ones where the answer is inconvenient, and
this is one of the inconvenient ones.

Pagan received Theravada Buddhism. From the Mon in the south, from Sri Lanka by
sea, and by whichever route the argument about 1057 eventually settles — but in
every version it arrived. What Pagan then did was make itself the largest single
monument to it in the world, on a plain that already believed.

Compare this corpus's Melaka record, whose single count is Sunni Islam. Melaka
also received its religion, from Pasai across the strait, and it also invested
enormously. The difference is what happened next: the ports of the archipelago
converted in Melaka's wake, through its merchants and its marriages, and Islam
reached the Moluccas and the southern Philippines by that route. The count is
one because something moved.

Nothing moved from Pagan. The Theravada tradition that later reshaped Cambodia
and Siam travelled through Sri Lanka and the Mon world, and the Burmese
reformist ordination lineages that did carry outward are later than this record.

## The other zero

`descendant_scripts` is also zero here, and for the reason this site now states
on half a dozen pages. The Burmese script was adapted at Pagan from the Mon
script, for a language of an entirely different family — which is exactly what
coding rule 3 counts, on the record of the script that was adapted. The Mon have
no page here.

The Angkor record in this region has the opposite outcome in the same pass: the
script adapted from it belongs to a parent that does have a page — Angkor's own
— and the Thai script counts there. Two records in one region, the same rule,
opposite results, and the difference is nothing about the polities at all. It is
about which pages have been written.
