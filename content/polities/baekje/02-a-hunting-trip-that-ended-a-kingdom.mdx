---
title: A hunting trip that ended a kingdom
drafted_from: hulbert-1905
phase: expansion

For its first quarter-century Baekje held its land as a guest of Ma-han,
the confederation whose king had granted On-jo his original settlement.
Baekje did not forget the debt outright: when it first raised a line of
breastworks against Ma-han's capital, Ma-han's own king sent a message
asking only "Did I not give you a hundred li of land? Why do you then
suspect me of hostile designs?" — and Baekje, "partly from shame,"
tore the barriers down again.

## A grant repaid with treachery

The peace did not last. Twenty-seven years after receiving his own land,
On-jo judged Ma-han weak enough to settle the relationship permanently.
He organized what was announced as a great hunting expedition, and under
its cover marched on the Ma-han capital and took it almost without a
fight. The source draws the comparison itself: as Wiman had once repaid
Gija's own kingdom of Joseon for its hospitality with betrayal, so now
On-jo repaid Ma-han's last king in exactly the same way. What had been a
tenant's plot became, in a single stroke, an independent kingdom holding
the ground its former patron once ruled.

## A rivalry with Silla begins

With Ma-han absorbed, Baekje's attention turned east, toward a Silla
still consolidating its own early kingdom. By the year 168 Baekje judged
itself strong enough to raid deep into Silla's western border and
carried a thousand captives home in triumph; Silla, unable to answer in
kind, could only write asking for their return. Fighting between the two
recurred across the following two centuries without either gaining a
decisive advantage, punctuated by occasional and always temporary
alliances against their shared northern neighbor, Goguryeo.

## A kingdom testing its own strength

By the early fourth century Baekje had absorbed Ma-han's territory, held
its own against Silla, and built enough of an army to matter in the wars
between its two larger neighbors — without yet having tested that army
against Goguryeo directly. That test, when it came, would carry Baekje's
own king further north than his kingdom had ever reached.
