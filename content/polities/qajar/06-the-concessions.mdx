---
title: Selling what was left
drafted_from: amanat-2017

The Qajar state's characteristic financial act was to grant a foreigner an
exclusive right in exchange for cash now and a share later, and it is worth
being precise about what kind of transaction that is, because it is not any of
the things pre-modern governments did for money.

It is not a monopoly in the old sense: the state did not operate these
monopolies, it sold them. It is not tribute, because nobody was formally
subordinate to anybody. It is not a toll. What was happening was the alienation
of future revenue by a government that needed cash in the current year — a
modern instrument, in a country with a medieval fisc, sold to buyers who
understood the instrument much better than the seller did.

The list is long and a few entries carry it. In 1872 the Reuter concession gave
a British subject the exclusive right to build railways and tramways, exploit
all mineral resources, run the customs, and found a national bank — Lord Curzon
called it the most complete surrender of the entire industrial resources of a
kingdom into foreign hands that had ever been dreamed of. Russian pressure and
domestic outrage killed it. In 1889 the Imperial Bank of Persia got the note
issue. In 1890 a British company got the monopoly on the purchase, sale and
export of all Iranian tobacco, which the country refused to let him keep. In
1901 William Knox D'Arcy got a sixty-year concession over oil across most of
the country for twenty thousand pounds and a sixteen per cent royalty.

From 1898 the customs were administered by Belgian officials, on the reasoning
that a neutral third party was preferable to either of the two powers. The
state borrowed from Russia to pay for a royal tour, and from Britain to pay
Russia.

By 1907 the two powers formalised what they had been doing informally: the
Anglo-Russian Convention divided Iran into a Russian sphere in the north, a
British sphere in the south-east and a neutral zone between, without consulting
the Iranian government and while it was in the middle of a constitutional
revolution.
