After 1303 the kingdom did what small states in that position do.

It appealed to Europe for a crusade, repeatedly and in writing, and no crusade
came. Het'um of Korykos wrote a book in French for a papal audience explaining
how an alliance with the Mongols could recover the Holy Land, which is the
kingdom's foreign policy compressed into an artefact and was read with interest
and acted on by nobody.

It accepted a Frankish dynasty. The Rubenid male line ran out in 1342 and the
crown passed to the Lusignans of Cyprus, who were Catholic, and who set about
the church union that two centuries of Armenian clergy had refused — which split
the kingdom internally at the moment it could least afford it. There were
revolts against the Latin kings by the population they were supposed to be
saving.

And it was raided, at intervals, by an enemy that did not need to hurry. Mamluk
armies came when it suited them. Towns were taken and not returned. The tribute
went up.

Sis fell in April 1375. The last king, Levon V, was taken to Cairo and
eventually released.

## What kind of ending that is

The kingdom made one large strategic decision in its history and made it early,
deliberately and — on the information available in 1247 — correctly.

Submitting to the Mongols before being attacked was the choice that saved every
polity that made it and destroyed every polity that did not. Hetum I sent his
brother across Asia to Karakorum, then went himself, then rode with Hulegu, and
for half a century it worked exactly as designed: privileges for his church,
protection from the south, and a share in the destruction of his enemies.

Then his ally lost a battle in Galilee, converted to his enemies' religion, and
stopped coming. The kingdom was left holding a commitment it could not
withdraw, on the frontier of the power that had beaten its protector, and it was
worked to pieces over the following century by an enemy that was never in any
particular danger from it.

There are a great many states that were destroyed for choosing wrongly. There
are very few that chose correctly, early, and were destroyed anyway because the
winner they had picked lost somewhere else.
