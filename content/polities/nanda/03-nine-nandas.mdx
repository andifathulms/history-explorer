Ask how many Nanda kings there were, and every tradition that survives
gives a different, confident, incompatible answer.

## Four sources, four dynasties

The Puranas count Mahapadma and eight sons: two generations, "the nine
Nandas" of later Sanskrit reference, reigning a combined century. The Jains
extend the same two generations to a hundred and fifty-five years. The
Buddhist Mahavamsa and Dipavamsa, and the Asokavadana on top of them, add
their own contradictory stories, none of which can be reconciled with the
others or, in most particulars, with anything independently verifiable.
Even the Kharavela inscription — a genuinely contemporary-adjacent
epigraphic source, cut on a rock at Udayagiri within a century and a half
of the dynasty's fall — mentions "Nanda Raja" twice without clarifying
which Nanda, or how many there were.

## What might explain the distortion

No single, obvious motive accounts for why every tradition mangles the same
nine decades so thoroughly. What can be said is that the dynasty's low
birth made it an uncomfortable subject for exactly the class of people who
wrote the surviving accounts down — Brahmanical, Jain and Buddhist
authors alike, each with reasons of their own to treat a barber's-son
dynasty as an aberration rather than a line worth recording carefully.

A usurpation that ends the age of high-born kings is the kind of story a
tradition remembers as a moral rather than as a chronology.

## What is left when the numbers are set aside

Strip out the irreconcilable regnal lengths and what remains is a
consistent core: an illegitimate founder, a reign built on an existing
kingdom's wealth and army rather than on new conquest, two generations of
rule, and an end by the same method as the beginning. The forty years
modern scholarship settles on is not a fact recovered from the sources so
much as the smallest span the surviving contradictions can be made to fit
inside — an estimate offered for want of anything better, on a dynasty
whose own certain history is shorter than the argument about how long it
lasted.
