Mali has no conqueror and no date anybody would defend closely, and its ending is
a series of humiliations rather than an event.

Niani was sacked by Songhai in 1545 and briefly held. Morocco destroyed Songhai
in 1591, and Mali outlived its destroyer by decades without being able to take
any advantage of it. The Bamana of Segu finished the empire in the seventeenth
century.

## An empire that outlived its own destroyer

The sequence at the end is worth setting out because it is genuinely strange.

Songhai took Mali's provinces, its trade and eventually its capital. Morocco
destroyed Songhai in 1591, with firearms carried across the Sahara. Mali was
still there — reduced, but still there — and could take no advantage of it
whatever, because the thing that had made the western Sudan worth fighting over
had already moved to the coast.

What finished it was neither of them. The Bamana of Segu, a people who had been
inside the empire, took what was left in the seventeenth century, and by then
there was not much to take.
