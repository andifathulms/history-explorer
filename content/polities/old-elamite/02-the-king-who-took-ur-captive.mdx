---
title: The king who took Ur captive
drafted_from: cameron-1936
phase: peak
---

Power passed from Awan to a rival dynasty at Shimashki, and the
transition was not gentle. As the Third Dynasty of Ur weakened, two
young and vigorous states attacked it from opposite directions at
once - Mari from the northwest, Shimashki from the east. An early
Shimashki king, Enbiluhhan, pushed down into the Elamite lowlands and
briefly occupied Susa itself, only to be met and defeated by Ur's own
king, Ibi-Sin, who boasted of overwhelming the land of Awan and the
cities of Adamdun and Susa in a single day and capturing Enbiluhhan
outright.

## A defeat answered by a second, decisive attack

Shimashki was not finished. Enbiluhhan's successor, Kindattu, occupied
Susa a second time, and this time his hold was absolute rather than
temporary. Where his predecessor had rushed to battle, Kindattu moved
carefully - restoring the temple of Inshushinak first, winning over
territories that had once answered to Ur, and only then turning his
full attention to Ibi-Sin himself. Ur's own king, meanwhile, was
fighting a second enemy simultaneously: Ishbi-Erra, a former officer
who had broken away in the north and was pressing toward Ur from that
direction. Ibi-Sin assured a frightened subordinate that the gods and
the Elamites themselves - whom he still, fatally, believed were
marching to his own aid - would guarantee his victory.

## A captured king, and an empire that did not survive him

The confidence was misplaced. Kindattu's own forces, not allies of
Ur's cause, took the city of Ur itself and carried Ibi-Sin off in
captivity - the event that ended the Third Dynasty of Ur outright.
Ishbi-Erra, denied the prize of Ur's own throne by Kindattu's more
decisive strike, instead built an entirely new kingdom of his own at
Isin. Kindattu, ruling from Anshan, held both Elam and the
recently-conquered Mesopotamian territories in a way no earlier ruler
of either Awan or Shimashki ever had - the single most consequential
act any king in this long Elamite sequence would accomplish.
