title: The only settled years Ibn Sina had
drafted_from: chi-4

This site's Ma'munid page ends with Ibn Sina refusing to go to Ghazna, leaving
Khwarazm before Mahmud's army arrived, and spending the following years moving
between the courts of western Iran — writing while employed as a physician and
an administrator, imprisoned once, in hiding more than once, and never
anywhere long.

In about 1024 he reached Isfahan, and Ala al-Dawla kept him until he died.

He was made a member of the court and served as physician and adviser, and by
several accounts as vizier; he accompanied the ruler on campaign, which is how
he came to die at Hamadan in 1037 on one of them. What the arrangement bought,
in a working life that had been interrupted about every three years, was
roughly thirteen consecutive ones.

The output is the reason a small Jibal principality has a page in this corpus.
The *Canon of Medicine* was substantially finished here, and it was taught in
European medical faculties into the seventeenth century. So was much of the
*Book of Healing*, the philosophical and scientific summa that carried
Aristotle, in Avicennan form, into Latin scholasticism.

And there is the *Danishnama-yi Ala'i* — the Book of Knowledge for Ala al-Dawla
— which he wrote for his patron, and wrote in Persian. That is the detail that
belongs to this page rather than to a history of philosophy. Ibn Sina's work is
in Arabic, as essentially all serious philosophy and science in this world was;
the Persian literary revival this site's Samanid and Saffarid pages describe was
poetry and history, not logic and metaphysics. The *Danishnama* is one of the
first attempts to do philosophy in Persian, and it exists because a Daylamite
prince in Isfahan wanted to read it and the terminology had to be invented to
let him.

The site's rating panel on this page shows four gaps and a longevity range. It
has no field for the fact that the most influential book in the history of
medicine was finished under this dynasty, or for the fact that Persian
philosophical vocabulary was assembled here to please one reader.
