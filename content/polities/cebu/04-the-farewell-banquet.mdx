The alliance did not survive Magellan by a week. Command passed to Duarte
Barbosa and Juan Serrano, and one of the new commanders threatened the
expedition's own slave interpreter, Enrique - a Malay who had served
Magellan since before the voyage began - telling him that his master's
death changed nothing: he would remain a slave, and would be flogged if
he refused to keep going ashore on the fleet's business. Enrique rose,
gave every appearance of accepting the order, and instead went to
Humabon with a different message: that the Spanish meant to leave very
soon, but that the king could seize their ships and cargo first if he
moved quickly. Pigafetta records plainly that the two of them arranged a
plot.

## Jewels that were never meant to be seen

On the morning of 1 May 1521, Humabon sent word to the Spanish commanders
that the gifts he had promised for the king of Spain were ready, and
invited the officers to a farewell dinner at his house. Twenty-four men
went ashore, among them the fleet's own astrologer; Pigafetta, still
swollen from a poisoned-arrow wound taken at Mactan, was one of the few
who stayed behind. Two men who had gone only partway turned back early,
uneasy at what they saw, and their unease had barely been reported to the
ships when the sound of shouting and screaming reached the anchorage.

## One survivor, and a ship abandoned

The fleet opened fire on the shore and closed in, and found Juan Serrano
alive but bound and wounded, shouting to them not to fire any more or he
would be killed. Everyone else who had gone to the banquet - Barbosa
included - was already dead, save the interpreter Enrique himself.
Serrano begged to be ransomed, but the ships, judging any rescue attempt
hopeless and suspecting further treachery, weighed anchor and sailed on
without him, so short-handed afterward that they scuttled one of the
fleet's three remaining ships rather than try to crew it. A king who
had sworn blood brotherhood with a foreign captain less than two weeks
earlier had, with the help of that captain's own slave, undone the
alliance entirely - the last the fleet that completed the
circumnavigation of the world would ever see of Cebu.
