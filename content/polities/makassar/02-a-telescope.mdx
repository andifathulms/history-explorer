Karaeng Pattingalloang was chancellor of Makassar from the 1630s until his death
in 1654, and he read Latin, Spanish and Portuguese.

He had Blaeu's globe. He ordered a telescope from the Netherlands and books to go
with it, corresponded about mathematics and astronomy, kept a library that
visitors described at length, and questioned every European who arrived about
the geography, the politics and the technology of wherever they had come from.
A Jesuit who met him wrote that he could have discussed the fine points of
European science with anyone in Europe.

The chapter is here because of what it does to a reader's default picture of this
region, and because this site cannot code it. There is no field for a court's
intellectual openness. `legitimation` reads `descent` and `divine-sanction` like
half the records on this site, and it says nothing.

## The pattern this record belongs to

It is the fourth time in the archipelago that the most important thing about a
page has turned out to have no field.

The Kediri record's second chapter is about the *Bharatayuddha* and the kakawin
tradition — three influence counts reading zero on a kingdom whose bequest was a
literature. The Ternate record's fourth is about Tidore, a defining relationship
that runs sideways in time and therefore cannot be an edge. The Medang record's
second is about Balaputradeva walking from a defeat in Java to a throne in
Sumatra, which is neither succession nor claim nor influence.

This is the fourth, and they are not all the same complaint. The first three are
about the *shape* of the schema — what a succession graph and three influence
counts can and cannot hold. This one is about what a polity page is for. A
chancellor's reading list is not an institution, it is not a measure, and it is
not evidence of anything except itself.

The site's answer, four times now, has been the same: put it in a chapter and
say the field is not there. That is a worse answer than having the field and a
much better one than pretending the absence means the thing did not happen.
