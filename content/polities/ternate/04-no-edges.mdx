title: A record with no thread
drafted_from: andaya-1993
phase: aside
---

Ternate has no edges. Nothing on this site comes before it, nothing comes after
it, and the succession graph for Maritime Asia — which now runs Srivijaya to
Singhasari to Majapahit, and Srivijaya to Melaka to Aceh — does not touch this
page at all.

That is ordinary. Twenty-five of the hundred and ninety records here have no
edge, and the project's own instructions are explicit that a polity with none is
complete: rankings include it, the reading pages include it, and no field on it
renders as a gap or a pending item. Rome entered this corpus with no sourced
edge to anything already in it.

It is worth being clear about *why* this one has none, because the reasons differ
across those twenty-five and they are not all the same kind of fact.

Some records have no edges because the corpus is thin around them — the Urartu
record's neighbours are mostly absent, and edges will appear when the pages do.
Some have none because the polity's predecessors and successors are outside this
site's scope entirely, which is the Kilwa case and the Great Zimbabwe case.

Ternate's is a third kind. The polity that defined it was its contemporary, not
its predecessor, and the schema has no way to say so; the polity that ended it
was a chartered company, and companies do not get pages here. Neither absence
would be repaired by writing more polity records of the ordinary sort. A Tidore
page would sit beside this one with no edge between them either, because
whatever Ternate and Tidore were to each other, it was not succession.

The blank is accurate. What it records is that this polity's most important
relationships ran sideways rather than forward, and that a graph of succession
is a picture of one thing rather than a picture of everything.
