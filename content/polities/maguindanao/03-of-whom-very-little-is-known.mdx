Kudarat was followed by his son, Dundang Tidulay, a sultan the
tarsila genealogies preserve almost nothing about beyond his own
name and his descendants - not a single campaign, treaty or building
project of his reign survives in the record, a striking silence
after a father whose wars had filled the same annals for decades.
His own son, Sultan Mohammed sa-Barahaman, ruled more visibly and,
by the account that survives, ruled peacefully, leaving behind
several children of his own.

## Two sons, and a question of age

Two of Barahaman's sons went on to become sultans in turn: Japar
Sadik Manamir and Dipatuan Anwar. When Barahaman died, Manamir was
declared his successor, but because he was still very young his
own succession was widely considered illegal - an irregularity that
gave his uncle, Kuda, grounds to contest it and set the stage for
the civil war that would consume the sultanate for a generation
after both of Barahaman's own sons were gone.
