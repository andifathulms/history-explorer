The Medes helped the Babylonians destroy Nineveh in 612 BC, ending Assyria, and
for the next sixty years are described by Herodotus as ruling an empire across
western Iran and eastern Anatolia until Cyrus overthrew Astyages in 550.

Whether that empire existed is genuinely contested. The Median state is known
almost entirely from Herodotus, writing a century later and at a distance, and
from Assyrian references to Median chiefdoms. Archaeological evidence for a
centralised Median administration is thin to absent, and a substantial body of
scholarship holds that the Medes were a tribal confederation that Greek and later
Persian sources retrospectively promoted into an empire because the Achaemenids
needed a predecessor to have succeeded.

This page keeps the record because the reference figure exists and is cited, and
because the polity is an edge in this site's Iranian thread. But the identity line
says the existence is disputed, and that is the point: this site's rules stop it
inventing numbers and citations, and they do not stop it inheriting a cited
figure for a polity whose reality is argued about. Provenance is not the same as
truth, and the verification worklist exists for exactly this gap.
