This record's span runs from 799 to 1538. Only three polities in the corpus are
longer, and two of them are empires. Its `military_basis` and `succession_rule`
are both null, its extent is null, and all three influence counts are null. A
reader scanning the rankings would find almost nothing here.

That is not a failure of research and it is worth setting out why, because the
Shirvanshahs are the case where the site's own instruments are least useful.

Coding rule 1 asks for the arrangement in force for most of a polity's life.
Across seven centuries this polity was an Abbasid governorship, an independent
principality, a Seljuk vassal, a tributary of the Ilkhanate, a Timurid client
and a Safavid dependency, and the way it raised troops in the tenth century
against Rus raiders coming down the Volga has nothing to do with the way it did
in the fifteenth. There is no arrangement that held for most of it. A value in
that field would be a claim about five hundred years no consulted source
describes.

What can be said is how the polity survived, and it is a method rather than an
institution. Submit early, before being attacked. Pay. Be useful on the frontier
to whoever is currently the power — against the Khazars for the caliphate,
against the Georgians and the Kipchaks for the Seljuks, against the Golden Horde
for Timur. Keep the mountains and the passes, which are expensive to take, and
be cheap enough to leave alone that nobody bothers. Do not fight for
independence, and do not lose the country.

This site has recorded pieces of that method elsewhere. The Artuqids
acknowledged nine sovereignties across two hundred and seventy-five years on a
rock at Mardin; the Trebizond emperors married daughters to whoever might
protect them; the Bavandids in the Caspian mountains persisted through six
centuries of everybody. The Shirvanshahs are the most successful version, and
what the fields on those pages have in common is a great many nulls.

There is no axis for durability that is not just the span, and the span is the
only thing on this page that is not empty. That is a real limit and it should be
stated on the record where it bites hardest.
