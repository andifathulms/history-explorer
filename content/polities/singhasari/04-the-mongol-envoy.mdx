title: The Mongol envoy
drafted_from: hall-2011

Kublai Khan sent envoys to Java demanding submission. Kertanegara, in the
account that has come down, had the last of them branded on the face and sent
back.

The Yuan expedition that followed — a thousand ships, in the Chinese accounts —
arrived in 1293. By the time it reached Java the king it had been sent to
punish was already a year dead.

The reasoning worth keeping is about what the sources are for. The Mongol
invasion of Java is documented in Chinese court records with dates and numbers,
and the events inside Java in the same years are documented in Javanese
chronicles that are legendary in structure and were compiled generations later.
The best-attested fact about the end of this polity is an expedition that
arrived too late to cause it.
