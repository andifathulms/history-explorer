The dated event, as against the legend, is 1222 at Ganter, where Ken Arok
defeated Kertajaya of Kediri. Kediri had been the dominant power in eastern
Java for a hundred and eighty years. After Ganter it was not, and the centre of
Javanese power moved to Tumapel, which the dynasty renamed Singhasari.

<Figure id="singosari-old" />

What changed is narrower than the political map suggests. Java in this period
is a rice economy: the Brantas and Solo valleys are among the most productive
wet-rice landscapes in the world. Kediri had that base and used it inland.
Singhasari inherited it and turned outward.

That is why its armies look nothing like a strait polity's. Srivijaya and,
later, Melaka fought with other people's ships — the *orang laut*, paid or
bound to serve. Singhasari raised its own men: the cultivators of the royal
domain owed service, and the army came up through the territorial lords rather
than being bought.

A state with a granary and a state with a customs house are different objects,
and Java's kingdoms are the first in these waters with the granary.
