That is what it lived on. Trebizond was the Black Sea end of the overland route
from Tabriz and Iran, and after the Mongols sacked Baghdad in 1258 a great deal
of the east-west traffic that had gone through Iraq moved north through Tabriz
to this coast instead. Genoese and
Venetian quarters were established in the city. Alum for the cloth industry,
silver from the Pontic mines, wine and hazelnuts went out; the empire took the
customs.

<Figure id="pontic-panorama" />

The Mongols, when they arrived, made it a tributary and left it alone, because
a functioning port at the end of their trade route was worth more than a ruined
one. That is the arrangement in miniature. For two and a half centuries this
state paid whoever was strongest and was allowed to continue.
