Trebizond's method after 1261 was tribute and marriage, in that order of
frequency.

The imperial title went first. In 1282, under pressure from the restored
Byzantine court, John II stopped styling himself emperor of the Romans and
became emperor of all the East, Iberia and Perateia. It is a formal concession
of the argument the state had been founded on, made in exchange for being left
alone as something smaller, and it worked: the empire lasted another hundred
and seventy-nine years after conceding it.

The marriages are the part the sources dwell on and they were policy. Grand
Komnenian daughters were married to Turkmen emirs, to Seljuk and later Ottoman
princes, to the Byzantine emperors and to Georgian kings, and the practice was
systematic enough that the beauty of Trapezuntine princesses became a
commonplace in Persian, Turkish and Greek writing alike. What it bought was a
web of relatives among the neighbours who might otherwise have taken the city,
and it bought about two hundred years.

The last attempt at it was John IV's, in the 1450s, and it is the strangest
alliance in this corpus. He married his daughter Theodora to Uzun Hasan, the
ruler of the Akkoyunlu — the Turkmen confederation of eastern Anatolia and
western Iran — in the hope that the Akkoyunlu army would come west if the
Ottomans came east. Then Constantinople fell in 1453, and Trebizond became the
last state anywhere still ruled by a Roman emperor.

It lasted eight more years. In the summer of 1461 Mehmed II brought an army
overland and a fleet along the coast, the city held out for about a month, and
David surrendered on 15 August. He and most of his family were executed in
Constantinople two years later. Uzun Hasan did not come.

This site records the end of the Roman empire on its Byzantine page in 1453,
which is the conventional date and is where the institution that mattered
stopped. The literal end is here, on a strip of coast at the other end of
Anatolia, eight years later, under a dynasty that had given up calling itself
Roman a hundred and eighty years before it stopped existing.
