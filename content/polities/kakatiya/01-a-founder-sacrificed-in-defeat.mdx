The family that would rule Telangana began as vassals of a greater
power. Beta I, an obscure petty chief, most likely accompanied his
Western Chalukya overlords on a campaign against the Chola capital of
Kanchi around 1052, and his descendants held the small fief of
Anumakonda under Chalukya authority for two more generations. Prola
II, the fourth ruler the inscriptions can name, expanded that fief
considerably and, in one campaign, captured a Chalukya prince named
Tailapa outright — then released him, impressed, the record says, by
his devout and amiable bearing. Even at the height of his own power,
Prola remained what his family had always been: a mandalika, a vassal
ruler owing allegiance to the Chalukya emperor at Kalyani.

## A reign that answered to no one

His son Rudradeva was the first Kakatiya ruler to hold real
independence, and inscriptions from his reign already describe a
prince who had transformed his father's petty principality into an
extensive dominion in his own right, humbling one rival chief after
another across Telangana. He moved his capital from Anumakonda to a
new stronghold at Warangal, a choice that would remain the dynasty's
seat for as long as it lasted.

## A death that nearly ended the dynasty in its first generation

Independence did not mean safety. The Yadava king Jaitugi, seeing an
opportunity while a threatened Hoysala invasion tied down his own
attention elsewhere, launched a sudden attack into Telangana soon
after 1195, and Rudradeva was killed in the fighting. One contemporary
chronicler describes the Yadava king celebrating the victory as though
he had performed a human sacrifice, offering up "the fierce Rudra" in
the same breath as any ritual offering. A Kakatiya record of the
period is candid about what followed: with the founder dead, "the
Kakatiya Royal Glory" — the dynasty's own fortune, described as a
woman abandoned — "found herself in a thorny jungle," with rival
chiefs converging to claim what was left. It would take a captivity,
and an unlikely release, before the kingdom found its footing again.
