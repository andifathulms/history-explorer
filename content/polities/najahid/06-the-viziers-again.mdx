title: The viziers again
drafted_from: smith-1974

The last Najahid century repeats the dynasty's own founding, which is either
irony or the ordinary working of the arrangement, depending on how much weight
one puts on the pattern.

The later sultans were young, or weak, or both, and the government passed to
their viziers — Mann Allah and after him others — who ruled Zabid in the
sultan's name and disposed of sultans who objected. It is precisely what the
Najahids had done to the Ziyadids in 1022, conducted by the same kind of men in
the same offices, and it took about the same length of time.

A state can run like that for a long time, and this one did. What it cannot
survive is a bad generation at the top of the household combined with pressure
from outside, and both arrived together.
