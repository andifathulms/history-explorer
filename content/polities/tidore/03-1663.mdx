Spain pulled its garrisons out of Maluku in 1663 to concentrate on defending
Manila, and Tidore was left facing the Dutch company without the ally it had held
for a hundred and forty-two years.

It should have been the end. It was the best thing that happened to the
sultanate in the seventeenth century.

## Why the company gave better terms

By 1663 Ternate had been a company dependency in all but name for a generation —
this corpus's Ternate record dates its ending to the treaty of 1683, and the
substance was gone well before the document. A company that had reduced one of
the two Malukan centres did not want the other one reduced too. It wanted a
counterweight: a second court with enough standing to keep Ternate's ambitions in
check and enough dependence to be manageable.

So Tidore negotiated, and got recognition of its eastern dependencies, a lighter
version of the extirpation obligation, and a status the company was careful not
to erode. Saifuddin, this record's `peak` ruler, reigned thirty years on that
footing.

## What it proves about the dualism

This record's first chapter, and Ternate's fourth, argue that the opposition
between the two islands was the organising structure of Maluku rather than a
rivalry between two neighbouring states — and that the two European empires which
arrived in 1512 and 1521 were recruited into it rather than imposing anything on
it.

The strongest evidence for that argument is here, and it is negative evidence.
Both Europeans were gone from the arrangement by 1663: the Portuguese expelled
from Ternate in 1575, the Spanish withdrawn from Tidore in 1663. The dualism kept
working anyway, with a Dutch company that had no interest in preserving it
finding that it needed to.

A structure that outlives the outsiders it recruited, and then makes a third
outsider maintain it, is not a rivalry produced by outsiders.

That is a claim about causation and this site has no field for it. What it has is
two records with no edge between them, identical in `military_basis` and adjacent
in every other field, and four chapters across the two of them explaining why the
blank between them is the point.
