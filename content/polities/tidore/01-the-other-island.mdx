Tidore is the island you can see from Ternate. The channel between them is
about two kilometres wide, and the two sultanates spent five hundred years
arranged against each other.

Almost everything about them is the same. The same crop, closely related
languages, courts of the same form, and a shared understanding of Maluku as a
place with two halves. What differed was which half you were, and that turned
out to be the most consequential fact about either of them.

## Where this account starts

1521, and the arrival of the *Victoria* — the last surviving ship of Magellan's
expedition, which reached Maluku eight months after Magellan was killed in the
Philippines, and loaded cloves here.

The sultanate is older than that. Its king list, like Ternate's, is not
securely datable, and a sultanate whose own beginnings are remembered rather
than documented has to be dated from the point where somebody outside it
started writing things down. Here that somebody was a Spanish crew with
eighteen survivors, on the last leg of the first voyage around the world, who
had come looking for exactly these islands and found them.
