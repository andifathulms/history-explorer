The great mosque at Demak is a Javanese building. It has a stacked pyramidal roof
of the kind that stood over Javanese shrines before Islam, no dome, no minaret,
and four main pillars — the *soko guru* — one of which tradition says was assembled
from wood chips by one of the *wali songo*, the nine saints.

It is the oldest substantially surviving mosque on Java and it is the physical
argument of this record.

## What Islamisation looked like here

The *wali songo* are the nine figures Javanese tradition credits with converting
the island: Sunan Kalijaga, Sunan Bonang, Sunan Giri, Sunan Gunungjati and the
rest, each with a tomb that is a pilgrimage site and a body of story attached.

What the stories consistently describe is accommodation. Sunan Kalijaga is
supposed to have used *wayang* — the shadow theatre, whose repertoire is the
Ramayana and the Mahabharata — to teach, and to have introduced gamelan
performance at the mosque as a way of drawing a crowd. The Javanese Islamic
tradition that came out of it kept the shadow plays, the gamelan, the calendar
partly recalculated rather than replaced, and a great deal of the older
vocabulary.

This corpus's Majapahit record's last chapter says the literature, the music, the
theatre and the religion continued after that court ended, and codes
`religions_carried: 1` for a Hindu-Buddhist Javanese tradition that moved east to
Bali. Both things are true and they are not in tension: some of it went to Bali
intact, and some of it stayed on Java inside the religion that replaced it.

## And what the coding says

`religions_carried` on this record is one, and its data comment describes Demak
carrying Islam inland to the rice country and the courts "by force" — the
campaigns of the 1520s and 1540s, one of which killed Trenggana.

Both mechanisms were running at once and the field can only hold the count. What
this chapter is for is to stop the count being read as the whole story: an army
took the courts, and a shadow-puppet repertoire took the villages, and the second
is why Javanese Islam looks the way it does and the first is why there was a
sultanate to look at.
