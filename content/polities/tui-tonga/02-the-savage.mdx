Kau-ulu-fonua's murderers fled the moment his war party landed, and
what followed was not a single battle but a chase down the whole
length of the Tui Tonga's own tribute network. Driven from 'Eua, the
killers ran to Ha'apai; driven from Ha'apai, to Vava'u; from Vava'u to
Niuatoputapu, and from there before the wind to Niuafo'ou, the most
remote of the northern islands. Still Kau-ulu-fonua followed. They
fled again to Samoa and were driven out from there too, then to 'Uvea,
and finally to Futuna - the western edge of everything the Tongans
knew, with no land beyond it to run to.

Every island in that chase was a name on the list of places that had
owed tribute to Takalaua. By the time Kau-ulu-fonua's canoes reached
Futuna, his pursuit of two old men had become, in effect, a
demonstration to the whole tribute network that the killing of a Tui
Tonga bought no one their freedom from it.

## The gods cannot even shield my back

At Futuna the fugitives had built a wall and waited, backed by
islanders who marvelled at Kau-ulu-fonua's reputation and told him he
owed his success not to his own courage but to the favour of the
gods. He mocked them for it - "Then I will leave my back to the gods
to defend, and myself defend my face" - and as he pressed through the
gate of the wall, one of the old murderers wounded him in the back all
the same. "The gods are fools," he is said to have cried, seizing the
man who had wounded him with his own hands and holding him until his
men broke through.

Both murderers were taken alive. Because they were old and had lost
their teeth, Kau-ulu-fonua had them chew hard, dry kava root before
him until their gums bled into the mixture, then made them strain and
knead it themselves and drank the whole draught alone. From that
single act came the name that stuck to him afterward - Kau-ulu-fonua
Fekai, "the savage" - and with the killing of his father's murderers
in Futuna, the pursuit that had run the length of the Tui Tonga's
tribute network was finished. He sailed home to Tonga a chief whose
reach no island in that network had escaped.
