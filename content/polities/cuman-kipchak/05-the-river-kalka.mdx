The blow that ended Kipchak independence came first from the south. A
small, disciplined Mongol army under Jebe and Subutai, fresh from
running down the survivors of the defeated Khwarazm-Shah's armies, was
ordered to bring the Kipchaks back under a single Turkic authority.
After defeating the Georgians and crossing the Caucasus, they met a
coalition of Alans, Kipchaks and their Circassian and Lesgian allies -
and broke it with characteristic diplomacy, persuading the Kipchaks to
abandon their allies on the argument that Mongols and Kipchaks were
brother Turks who had no real quarrel with each other.

## An old enemy's warning goes unheeded

Konchak's own son Yury, ruling alongside a khan named Kotyan, saw
through the maneuver too late to save the coalition, but in time to
flee to the Russian frontier and beg help from the very princes his
own family had spent generations raiding. The Mongols tried the same
trick again, telling the Russians they had no quarrel with them either
and only wished to punish their own runaway slaves. The Russians
refused the offer - and were maneuvered instead into an unfavorable
position on the river Kalka, where the combined Russian-Kipchak force
was completely destroyed. The Mongol army withdrew north around the
Caspian and home, its work in the region done for the moment.

## A steppe reorganized under a new name

The respite did not last. When the Mongols returned in greater force,
Kotyan fled west to Hungary with a large following of his own people,
while another khan, Yunus, crossed the Danube to fight for the Latins
still holding Constantinople against the Greeks. Scattered Kipchak
resistance held out for a time on the Volga and in the Caucasus, but
the whole of the steppe that had been theirs passed into Mongol hands
and was reorganized as the Kipchak Khanate - the polity better known
to history as the Golden Horde - under Batu, with a new capital at
Sarai on the Volga. The grassland the Kipchaks had fought Russians,
Georgians, Bulgarians and Greeks over for a century and a half now
answered to a single Mongol dynasty instead.
