---
title: The state he built
drafted_from: gammer-1994
phase: peak

Shamil's reputation is as a guerrilla leader, and Gammer's book is largely an
argument that this understates him. What held out against the Russian empire for
a quarter of a century was not a resistance movement. It was a state, built
deliberately, in country that had never had one.

He divided the imamate into districts under *naibs* — deputies he appointed and
removed, drawn from men who owed their position to him rather than to a lineage
— with *mudirs* over groups of them. He established sharia courts under qadis
and put them above the village assemblies. He built a treasury with a defined
revenue: zakat under the law he had imposed, and the fifth of booty from raiding
across the Russian lines, which this site's revenue field records as `plunder`
in the strict sense the coding rules require.

And he built an army that was not a levy. The *murtaziqa* were mounted men
maintained permanently under arms at community expense — each group of
households required to equip and support one — answering to the imam rather than
to their village, which is why this record carries `standing-professional`
alongside `conscript`. He organised units by hundreds and thousands, issued
orders in writing, kept a chancery in Arabic, and had cannon cast locally by
deserters and prisoners.

The corpus has recorded this shape before, in the Arabian region. The Emirate of
Diriyah on this site's Najd record is a doctrine and a family in oasis country
that carried `land-tax` and `plunder` for exactly the same reasons. The
difference is that Diriyah expanded and this did not; the imamate never held
anything but mountains, and its entire institutional effort went into making
mountains defensible.

The cost of all of it was borne by the population it governed, and Gammer does
not soften that. Naibs were resented, the taxation was heavy, the enforcement of
the law was severe, and by the 1850s a good deal of the imamate's difficulty was
that the communities it was defending had stopped wanting to pay for the defence.
