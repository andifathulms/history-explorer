Matthias's death opened an immediate contest for Hungary's own vacant
throne. Frederick and Maximilian put themselves forward as candidates,
citing the succession compact already agreed with Matthias, against a
field that included the dead king's own illegitimate son, Bohemia's
King Ladislaus, and Ladislaus's own brother Albert. Despite
Maximilian's evident talent and energy, the Hungarian diet chose
Ladislaus on 15 July 1490 - but Maximilian refused to accept the
rejection quietly. Rallying his father's own Austrian subjects, he
drove the occupying Hungarian garrisons out of Austria in six weeks
and pushed on into Hungary itself, exploiting the usual factional chaos
that followed any new king's accession. He captured Székesfehérvár,
the traditional coronation and burial city of Hungary's own kings, and
was preparing to march on Buda when a mutiny among his own troops
forced him to fall back into Austria, leaving only a garrison behind.

## A peace at Pressburg

Neither side could press its advantage further: Maximilian's own
appeals to the imperial diet for reinforcement went nowhere, giving
Ladislaus time to stabilize his new kingdom, and Maximilian - his
attention increasingly pulled toward events in Brittany - eventually
agreed to negotiate rather than continue a campaign with no clear end.
The two met at Bratislava and settled generous terms: Ladislaus kept
the Hungarian crown and formally renounced any claim to the Austrian
territory Matthias had once conquered, while Maximilian received the
honorary title of King of Hungary, a payment of a hundred thousand
ducats toward his own war costs, and a renewed family compact
entailing the Hungarian succession on Frederick's own line should
Ladislaus die without a son. Between Matthias's death and Maximilian's
own energy and skill, Frederick recovered the Austrian homeland he had
lost for more than a decade, gained financial compensation on top of
it, and was finally relieved of a crisis his own obstinacy and
indolence had done so much to create in the first place.
