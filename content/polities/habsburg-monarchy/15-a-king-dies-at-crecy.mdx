John of Bohemia's campaign against Louis of Bavaria succeeded where
his campaign for Tyrol had failed. Working through Pope Clement VI,
who had already deposed one uncooperative elector to clear the way,
John assembled enough support among the archbishops and the Saxon and
Bohemian votes to have Louis declared deposed at a meeting in 1346 and
his own eldest son Charles chosen as rival emperor in his place. Louis
fought back with real success at first, supported by Albert and a
German party that resented the Pope's high-handed interference in an
imperial election, and drove Charles back into Bohemia itself - a
kingdom Charles had only just inherited, since his own father died
that same year fighting for the French crown at Crécy, killed
according to legend by his own choice, riding blind into the thick of
the battle rather than watch it from safety.

## An unexpected vacancy

Louis was on the verge of pressing his advantage into the heart of
Bohemia when an apoplexy killed him suddenly in October 1347, handing
Charles the imperial crown almost by accident after years of fighting
for it. A rump faction of electors tried briefly to raise an alternate
candidate, but he died within the year, and Charles was confirmed
emperor by unanimous vote soon after. Albert, who had spent the
preceding contest as one of Louis's most committed supporters, judged
the political weather correctly and switched his allegiance the moment
Louis was gone: he personally helped broker a reconciliation between
the houses of Bavaria and Luxembourg, and used his new standing with
Charles to settle the Tyrol question for good, securing the emperor's
own confirmation of Margaret's controversial divorce and remarriage,
and arranging the investiture of Tyrol for a Bavarian prince in
exchange for a useful vote at the next imperial election. A dispute
that had poisoned relations between Austria and Bohemia for over a
decade closed not with another battle, but with the two former rivals
trading favors as if the war over a single duchess's marriage had
never happened.
