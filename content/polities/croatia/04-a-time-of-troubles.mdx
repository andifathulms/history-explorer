Zvonimir died in 1089 or 1090 - legend, Fine notes, has him killed in a
brawl during a council, though the story cannot be confirmed. He left
a single young son by his Hungarian wife, and that boy, the last of the
Trpimirovic dynasty Tomislav had founded, died within the year. With
him died the line itself, and with the line died the one thing that had
held the kingdom's centralizing nobles-versus-crown tensions in check.
Zvonimir's own widow tried to hold the throne, but she was unpopular
with a nobility that had already resented her husband's policies and
was now free to reassert the local independence he had spent his reign
curbing.

## A Hungarian regency, and a king who pushed it out

Her brother, the King of Hungary, intervened at once to protect her
interests - and his own ambitions along with them - occupying much of
Croatia and part of Dalmatia before an attack on Hungary itself by
raiders from the steppe forced a partial withdrawal. The Hungarians
kept inland Pannonian Croatia, organizing it as a separate banovina
under Almos, the king's own nephew, from 1091 to 1095, and
establishing a bishopric at Zagreb answerable to a Hungarian
archbishop rather than to Split. What remained of Croatia - Dalmatia
and whatever of the interior Almos's banovina had not absorbed - was
taken over by a ruler named Peter, based at Knin, who by 1095 had
recovered enough ground to expel Almos outright.

## The last independent king

Peter's independence did not survive the new Hungarian king, Koloman,
who succeeded to the Hungarian throne at the end of 1095 and sent his
armies back into Pannonian Croatia the following year. Peter was
killed fighting them on the mountain later renamed Petrova gora in his
memory - the last king to rule an independent Croatia. Koloman
followed the victory by occupying Biograd in 1097, giving Hungary the
port on the Adriatic it had long wanted, and the remaining Dalmatian
cities, alarmed at the Hungarian advance, turned instead to Venice for
protection. A kingdom that six years earlier had still been ruled from
within itself now had no king of its own left to rule it at all.
