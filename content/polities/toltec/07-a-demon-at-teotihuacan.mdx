By the 1040s the strife between Quetzalcoatl's own followers and the
Tezcatlipoca faction that had simmered since Acxitl's birth erupted into
open violence inside Tollan itself. A rival religious sect was admitted
into the capital by partisans of Tezcatlipoca even as the king's own
forces were away campaigning elsewhere, and fighting between the rival
factions in the city's own streets left its temples and palaces nearly
in ruins. War between the allied kingdoms and a fresh outbreak of
pestilence and famine followed in the same years, a run of disasters the
chronicles date to between 1040 and 1047.

## An assembly that begged for mercy and received a demon instead

Convinced the gods themselves had turned against them, the kingdom's
priests, nobles and wise men convened a great propitiatory assembly at
Teotihuacan, the ancient holy city where prayers had always been heard.
What answered them, the tradition records, was no reassurance. A demon
of huge size with long bony fingers appeared dancing among the
assembled crowd, seizing Toltecs at random and dashing them dead at his
feet; those present found themselves unable to flee even as hundreds
died around them. The apparition returned a second time in a different
shape with the same result, and a third time as a beautiful pale child
sitting on a hilltop - until onlookers who approached discovered its
head was a mass of corruption whose stench killed on contact. A final
apparition told the assembly plainly that the gods would hear no more
petitions, and that only flight could save the Toltecs from complete
destruction.

## Invaders arriving as an old truce expired

Whatever the apparitions' own nature, the disasters they seemed to
foretell were concrete enough. Toltec nobles had already begun
emigrating from the country in growing numbers even before any formal
decision to abandon it was made, and a ten-year truce between Acxitl's
government and rebel forces to the north was, by 1058, nearing its end.
Bands from the surrounding regions - loosely grouped by the chronicles
under the name Chichimec, though which specific peoples were involved
cannot now be traced with confidence - took advantage of Tollan's own
internal dissension to press into Toltec territory from multiple
directions at once, preparing a war the weakened kingdom was in no
position to win outright.
