`military_basis` on this record was null until this pass. It reads `tribal-levy`
now, which is accurate and does not describe the thing worth describing.

The Liao emperors each raised an *ordo*: a standing household guard, drawn from
tribal levies and from captured populations, with its own herds, its own
territory and its own revenue. It went to war with him, and when he died it did
not pass to his successor. It became the permanent establishment of his tomb.

So each emperor built an army from scratch, and the dead accumulated armies.

## What that is for

It looks like waste and it is a solution to a specific problem. A steppe ruler
whose guard passes intact to his heir has handed the heir's rivals a target and
handed the guard commanders a vote in the succession — which is exactly what
happened repeatedly to this corpus's Yuan record, where guard commanders murdered
an emperor at the summer camp in 1323.

An ordo that dissolves into a tomb establishment cannot be inherited, cannot be
bargained with, and cannot make an emperor. The cost is that every reign begins
with the ruler recruiting.

`succession_rule` on this record is null anyway, and the comment says why: there
is a Chinese-style heir apparent in the record and a steppe assembly behind it,
and the empress dowagers of the Xiao consort clan — which supplied every Liao
empress and much of the officer corps — decided several transitions by force. The
ordo did not fix the succession. It made sure the army was not the thing that
decided it.

## And two revenue systems

This record's `revenue_basis` used to read `tribute` alone, which was here for the
Song payments the second chapter is about, and which left out the other half of
the arrangement the first chapter describes.

The Southern Administration taxed a settled Chinese and Bohai population on
agricultural land, in the ordinary way, and that population was the majority of
the empire's people. A record whose entire subject is governing two things at
once should not have been coded for only one of them. It reads `tribute` and
`land-tax` now.
