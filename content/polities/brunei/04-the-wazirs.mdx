`succession_rule` on this record is null, and the reason is not that the sources
are silent. It is that the office of sultan was one part of a structure and the
sources describe the structure.

Brunei was governed by ranked noble houses. Four great officers — the *wazirs* —
stood between the sultan and everything else: the Pengiran Bendahara, the
Pengiran Di-Gadong, the Pengiran Pemancha, the Pengiran Temenggong. Below them a
graded nobility of *pengiran* held rights over named districts and over the
people in them, transmitted by inheritance.

The sultan came from the ruling house. Which member, and whether he was replaced,
was worked out among the wazirs and the senior pengiran, and the record shows
contested successions, depositions and periods of two claimants.

## The comparison one page over

This corpus's Johor record's fourth chapter is about a bendahara — the same
office, the same word, the same position in a Malay court — who in 1699 had the
sultan killed on the way to the mosque and took the throne, ending the Melaka
royal line.

And this corpus's Melaka record's fifth chapter is about the bendahara there, the
office held for most of the fifteenth century by one family, whom the *Malay
Annals* show choosing between claimants and restraining rulers. That record's
`succession_rule` is null too, and its comment says coding it `nomination` would
credit a system to what the sources record as an influence.

Three records, three bendaharas, three nulls in the same field. What the field is
failing to hold is the same thing in every case: a Malay sultanate is not a
monarchy with ministers, it is an arrangement among houses in which one house
supplies the ruler, and a succession field that offers `primogeniture`,
`nomination`, `election` and six others has no value for that.

The nearest is `factional`, and it is not right either — it implies breakdown, and
this was the working order rather than the failure of one.

That is a gap in the vocabulary rather than in the evidence, and three records in
one region carrying the same null for the same reason is roughly the volume at
which this site has twice before gone and changed the vocabulary. It has not
here, because `investiture` and `matrilineal` were both added on a corpus-wide
audit and this observation has only been made in one region.
