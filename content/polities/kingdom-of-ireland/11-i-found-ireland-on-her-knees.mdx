By 1782 the Irish Parliament, though it represented only the
Protestant Ascendancy and answered still to laws passed at
Westminster it had no power to amend, had behind it a force the
British government could not safely dismiss: an armed Volunteer
movement, originally raised to defend the country against French
invasion during the American war, whose numbers and organization had
given the demand for legislative independence a weight mere argument
never could. A Volunteer convention at Dungannon adopted resolutions
asserting Irish independence, drafted in cooperation between Henry
Grattan and his old rival Henry Flood - a union of purposes striking
enough on its own that observers took it as proof the government's
old strategy of dividing Irish opinion by faction had finally failed.

## An escort through the ranks

The government yielded rather than face a nation "united and in
arms." On 16 April 1782, a body of Volunteers drawn up before the
Parliament House in Dublin parted their ranks to let Grattan pass
through to move the declaration of independence himself. What followed
is among the most quoted passages in Irish parliamentary history:
"I am now to address a free people... I found Ireland on her knees...
I have traced her progress from injuries to arms, and from arms to
liberty. Spirit of Swift, spirit of Molyneux, your genius has
prevailed! Ireland is now a nation." He closed with two Latin words -
esto perpetua, may it last forever - that history would not, in the
end, grant him.

## A freedom exercised by very few

The Parliament that had just won this independence remained what it
had always been: purely Protestant, narrowly propertied, and answerable
to an electorate that excluded the great majority of the population it
governed. Grattan himself believed the achievement carried within it
the seeds of further reform - that an independent Irish public opinion
would go on to widen the franchise and remove the remaining
disqualifications in time. Whether it would have, left to itself,
became one of Irish history's enduring counterfactuals; events within
two decades would not leave it the chance to find out.
