Kazan fell in October 1552. From that month the whole Volga from the forest to the
delta was open to one power, and the balance that had kept Astrakhan alive was
over.

## What the fall of Kazan changed

It was not that Moscow had become suddenly stronger. It was that the river had
become continuous.

A Russian army could now be assembled on the upper Volga, put into boats, and
delivered fifteen hundred kilometres downstream with its guns and provisions
intact, to a place where no fortified point remained in the way. Nothing on the
lower river could stop a fleet coming down it, and nothing on the steppe could
feed an army marching the same distance overland — which was why the delta had
been safe from the north for three centuries.

Ivan IV understood what he now held. He took the title tsar of Astrakhan in
official documents from 1552, four years before he had the place.

## The client

Yamgurchi, khan at the time of Kazan's fall, made his submission to Moscow and
then broke it, turning back to the Crimea and the Ottomans, who had every reason
to want the delta out of Russian hands.

In 1554 a Russian expedition came down the river, drove him out, and installed
Dervish Ali — a Chinggisid who had spent years in Russian service — as khan on
terms: tribute, a Russian voice in his affairs, and free passage for Russian
merchants and fishermen.

It was the oldest arrangement in the khanate's history, with a new patron. Every
khan of Astrakhan had held his throne on somebody's sufferance, and the throne had
survived each time because the patron changed. The men who accepted Dervish Ali in
1554 had no particular reason to think this patron would be different.

## Why it was different

Because Moscow did not want a khan. It wanted the river mouth, and it had just
demonstrated at Kazan what it did with a city it wanted: took the walls, removed
the dynasty, garrisoned the place and taxed it directly.

Dervish Ali seems to have understood this within a year. He opened negotiations
with the Crimea and the Ottomans, which was the only move available to him and the
one that ended the khanate.
