For roughly a century after Gunung Jati's death in 1568, Cirebon governed
itself, which is not something every Muslim state on Java's coast in this
period managed to do.

## Outliving the power that helped found it

Demak, whose sultan had given Gunung Jati his connections and his backing,
did not outlast the sixteenth century as a unified state: it broke apart in
succession disputes within a generation of its own greatest ruler's death.
Cirebon, founded as something close to a junior partner in Demak's own
expansion, was not dragged down with it. Its ruling house's separate claim
to religious standing, rather than to a share of Demak's own inheritance,
meant Cirebon's legitimacy did not depend on Demak's continuing to exist.

Banten, the sister foundation planted by Gunung Jati's own family further
west, took the opposite path and grew into the larger and more powerful
state of the two — a major pepper entrepot with its own extensive trade
network reaching Europe and China both. Cirebon did not compete with Banten
on that scale and did not try to. It held its own coastal plain, taxed its
own port traffic, and stayed independent by staying, in relative terms,
modest.

## What independence looked like

Graaf and Pigeaud's account of this period is thinner than for the
generation of the founder, which is itself informative: an independent
Cirebon that was not being fought over or absorbed by a stronger neighbour
generated less to write about than the wars and successions that mark most
of this coast's early Islamic history. Continuity, in the source record, is
frequently silence.

What silence in this specific case most likely means is a working
settlement: Cirebon's rulers maintained the religious prestige inherited
from Gunung Jati, administered a coastal territory that fed and financed
them adequately without inviting conquest, and stayed out of the larger
contests then developing between Banten in the west and the rising inland
power of Mataram to the east. That position could not last once Mataram
decided the north coast was worth having outright, and the next chapter is
about what happened when it did.
