Hideyoshi died on 15 September 1598, having spent his final arrangements
trying to guarantee a future he would not live to oversee. He married
his six-year-old son Hideyori to Ieyasu's own granddaughter and
appointed five senior ministers as the boy's guardians, hoping the
marriage tie would bind Japan's most powerful remaining vassal to his
heir's cause rather than against it.

## A settlement that assumed too much loyalty

The arrangement rested on a wager: that Ieyasu, the one man capable of
overturning everything Hideyoshi had built, would honour a family
connection and a guardian's oath over his own ambition. The wager held
for less than two years. As the coalition of regents meant to protect
Hideyori's inheritance fractured into factions, Ieyasu moved to secure
the whole country for himself rather than merely to protect a share of
it for someone else's son.

## A general who answered his own diviners

When Ieyasu marched west from Edo on 1 October 1600 to confront the
coalition still fighting in Hideyori's name, his own diviners warned him
the road west was inauspicious. He answered that he would open it by
knocking. Along the march, someone offered him a persimmon; as it fell
into his hand he remarked that Ogaki - a fortress on his route - had
just fallen into his hand the same way, then distributed the fruit to
his own attendants as a good omen. It was the gesture of a commander who
had already decided the campaign's outcome before the armies met.

## One purpose against many

At Sekigahara, Ieyasu's disciplined force of a single mind faced a
coalition army nearly twice its size but split among commanders with
competing interests and no shared strategy. The imbalance proved
decisive. Ieyasu's victory did not immediately end the Toyotomi house -
Hideyori remained in Osaka Castle for another fifteen years - but it
destroyed the coalition that had fought to preserve his father's own
settlement, and left the country's actual mastery in the hands of a man
who would formally claim the shogunal title in his own right three
years later.
